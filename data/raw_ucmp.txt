Q1. Non-traditional machining processes are used when:
A) Conventional machining is easy
B) Material is too hard/brittle or geometry too complex for conventional methods
C) Cost needs to be minimized always
D) MRR needs to be very high always
Answer: B

Q2. Non-traditional machining processes are also known as:
A) Conventional machining

D) Forming processes
Answer: B

Q3. In conventional machining, material removal occurs mainly by:
